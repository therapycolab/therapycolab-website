Common Questions

Quick Answers

Everything else you might want to know before reaching out.

Is there a long-term commitment?+

No. All memberships are month-to-month. There are no long-term leases or multi-year commitments — you scale up or down as your practice evolves.

How long does the approval process take?+

Most providers hear back within 2–3 business days of submitting their application. We keep the process straightforward and respectful of your time.

What if I'm not sure which plan to choose?+

Start with a tour. We'll walk you through the options based on your practice volume and goals — no pressure, just clarity. You can always adjust your tier later.

Do I need to bring my own supplies or equipment?+

No. Every room is fully furnished and equipped. Show up ready to see clients — we handle the rest. Shared materials, amenities, and high-speed Wi-Fi are included with every membership.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my membership tier?+

Yes. You can adjust your tier at any time as your practice grows or changes. Just reach out and we'll update your membership for the next billing cycle.
