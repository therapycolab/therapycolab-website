About the Space

A Collaborative Workspace Built for Providers

Therapy Colab was created by clinicians who understood the gap between a solo home practice and the cost of a traditional private office. We built a space that bridges that gap—professional, warm, and designed to foster community without the isolation that often comes with independent practice.

Every room is thoughtfully furnished for therapeutic work: sound-attenuating walls, natural lighting, comfortable seating, and the privacy your clients deserve. Shared amenities like a welcoming reception area, kitchenette, and high-speed Wi-Fi mean you arrive ready to work—nothing more to set up.

Whether you're just starting out, expanding your caseload, or looking for a professional home for your established practice, Therapy Colab grows with you.

Who It's For

Built for the Full Spectrum of Behavioral & Allied Health

Behavior Analysts & ABA Providers

BCBAs and ABA providers who need professional, well-equipped rooms for assessments, parent training, and one-on-one sessions—a welcoming clinical space that supports behavior-analytic work and the families you serve.

Mental Health Therapists & Counselors

LCSWs, LMFTs, LPCs, and psychologists who want a professional, private office without a long-term commercial lease. Ideal for solo practitioners ready to see clients in person—full-time or part-time.

Speech-Language Pathologists & Occupational Therapists

Dedicated rooms with the space and acoustics needed for speech therapy, OT evaluations, and sensory work. A professional setting that inspires confidence in clients and families alike.

Early-Career Clinicians

New to private practice? Our Foundation membership is designed to let you build your caseload incrementally—with a professional address and office space available when you need it, without a prohibitive upfront commitment.

Growing Therapy Teams

Group practices, behavioral health startups, and multi-disciplinary teams that need reliable, bookable space for multiple providers. Our Clinical Partner tier scales to meet your scheduling demands.
